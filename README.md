# CarePlus Hospital Appointment Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
The **CarePlus Hospital Appointment Analysis Dashboard** is an Excel-based data analytics project that explores hospital appointment transactions. It is designed to help understand appointment trends, department performance, waiting times, cancellations, patient satisfaction, and the overall patient experience.

## Objectives
- Analyze appointment volume over time.
- Compare appointments across hospital departments.
- Identify departments or cases with longer patient waiting times.
- Review appointment cancellation patterns.
- Understand the age distribution of patients.
- Evaluate patient ratings and satisfaction.
- Highlight operational opportunities to improve the appointment process and patient experience.

## Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables and summarized tables
- Charts and dashboard-style visualizations
- Data analysis and reporting

## Dataset
The workbook includes an `Appointment_Transactions` sheet with approximately 807 appointment records and fields covering:

- **Appointment details:** Appointment ID, date, time, month, quarter, and weekday
- **Hospital details:** Hospital ID and hospital name
- **Patient details:** Patient ID, gender, and age
- **Clinical/service details:** Department, doctor, consultation type, and diagnosis category
- **Appointment outcomes:** Appointment status and follow-up indicator
- **Operational measures:** Waiting time and consultation duration
- **Payment and feedback:** Appointment fee, insurance, payment mode, and patient rating

The workbook also contains analysis sheets for appointment trends, department-wise appointments, waiting time, cancellation rate, age groups, patient satisfaction, operational improvements, and patient experience.

## Dashboard and Analysis Areas
1. **Appointment Trend** – reviews appointment counts across time periods.
2. **Department-wise Appointment Analysis** – compares appointment volume by department.
3. **Waiting Time Analysis** – examines average waiting time and highlights potential bottlenecks.
4. **Appointment Cancellation Rate** – explores cancellation patterns.
5. **Patient Age Group** – summarizes the distribution of patients by age group.
6. **Patient Satisfaction** – reviews average patient ratings.
7. **Operational Improvements** – supports identifying areas where scheduling and service processes may be improved.
8. **Patient Experience** – summarizes appointment outcomes related to the patient journey.

## Workflow
1. Review the appointment transaction data.
2. Check data fields and data quality before analysis.
3. Summarize records using Excel analysis tools such as PivotTables.
4. Compare appointment volume, waiting time, cancellation, and satisfaction measures.
5. Present the findings in a dashboard/report format.
6. Use the results to suggest practical operational improvements.

## Potential Business Value
This analysis can help hospital operations teams:
- Monitor appointment demand and department workload.
- Identify waiting-time issues that may need investigation.
- Track cancellations and review scheduling practices.
- Understand patient feedback.
- Make more informed decisions about appointment scheduling and service improvements.

## How to Use
1. Open the Excel workbook.
2. Go to `Appointment_Transactions` to review the source data.
3. Open the relevant analysis sheets to explore each topic.
4. Use the dashboard/report view to communicate findings.
5. If PivotTables or charts are used, refresh them after changing the source data.

## Limitations
- The workbook contains operational and patient-related fields; use anonymized data when sharing publicly.
- Findings describe the data in this workbook and should not be treated as proof of cause and effect.
- Recommendations should be validated with hospital staff and additional context before implementation.
